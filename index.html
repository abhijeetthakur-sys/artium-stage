Three programmes from the Talent Development Department
Department

An invitation-only talent development programme for Artium's most exceptional students — identified through AE tagging or showcase performance. It's not open to all. It is earned.

ATDP runs 12 structured grooming sessions across four tracks: Song Performance Labs with Jaydeep Bagwadkar, The Carnatic Grooming Series with Sidhi Krishnamoorthy, Social Media for Musicians with Shrey Jadav, and The Nuances of Western Vocals with Rachel Dohling. Live attendance is required. Post-session tasks are assigned. Every newsletter in this archive was born from something ATDP made happen.
Original Music

Artium's original music label. Open to both students and teachers/AEs. For Students, Superstars winners and exceptional ATDP students get priority. Teachers & AEs release through the Teacher Series. 50+ Artium Originals already out. More in the pipeline through 2027.

Every release is a real one — professionally recorded, with artwork, distributed to Spotify, Apple Music, and 40+ platforms. Romi Roy's Suno Dayal was the first Teacher Series release. Vinisha Vinoth's Vaanil Megame — releasing 29 May 2026 — makes her the first under-16 artist to record a South Artium Original. Ten more are in the pipeline through March 2027.
Live Performances

Artium's live performance series. A room, a small stage, a camera, and the moment a song stops being rehearsed and becomes real. Mumbai was first.

Every performance at unMute is professionally shot and edited. Special tracks of each song are produced. Videos roll out on YouTube and Instagram in the weeks after each edition. Bangalore was where it all began, followed by Mumbai and Chennai, and now we're spreading our wings to Hyderabad, while also taking our first step onto the International stage with the very first global edition of unMute. Stay tuned.
