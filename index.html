Three programmes from the Talent Department
Department

The internal programme that gives Artium's own performers the same quality of grooming, recording, and stage opportunities that Artium gives its students.

ATDP is not a course. It is a structured space where Artium employees who also perform get professional development in music, stage presence, and recording. Every newsletter in this archive was born from something ATDP made happen. Grooming sessions, original releases, and live shows — all under one roof.
Original Music

The label side of ATDP. Songs that deserved to exist, recorded properly and released on every platform. Romi Roy's Suno Dayal was the first. More are coming.

Not demos. Not rough recordings. Real releases — with artwork, Spotify listings, and stories told well. Artium Originals exists because there are compositions inside our teachers that have never been recorded, and that thread should not stay that thin.
Live Performances

Artium's live performance series. A room, a small stage, a camera, and the moment a song stops being rehearsed and becomes real. Mumbai was first.

No ticketing. No production spectacle. Just the moment music stops being practiced and becomes performed. We record it, put it on YouTube, and tell the story in a newsletter. unMute Live is Artium performing in public for the first time.
